A sweet kind of hospitality

Born from culture.
Made with heart.

House of Glory brings the bright flavors and generous spirit of Miami, Cuba, Colombia, and beyond to Southern Oregon.

Founded by husband-and-wife duo Manny and Zach Velazquez, our historic downtown café is a place to slow down, share something beautiful, and feel known. Nearly everything is organic, nothing is ordinary, and every treat is made in house in small batches.
