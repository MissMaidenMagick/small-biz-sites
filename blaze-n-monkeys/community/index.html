Fourth of July

A festival built around food—and giving.

Blaze-N-Monkey's has helped organize the annual Grants Pass Fourth of July Food Truck Festival and Food Drive at Reinhart Volunteer Park, bringing multiple vendors together while collecting donations for the Josephine County Food Bank.

Community connections

Partnerships beyond the festival.

Public sources also connect Blaze-N-Monkey's with local library initiatives, food-bank fundraisers, winery events, and other Rogue Valley gatherings.

In 2026, Patrick and Misti Hoadley were recognized with a Community Spirit Award—an acknowledgment that fits the way the truck has shown up for Grants Pass.
