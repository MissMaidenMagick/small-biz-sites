Before you head over

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning dinner, karaoke, or a patio night? Here are the answers people ask for most.

Still curious? Call (541) 244-1678
What are Flespy’s hours?

Flespy’s is open seven days a week from 11 AM to midnight.

When does the kitchen close?

The kitchen serves the full menu until midnight every day.

Is the full menu available all day?

Yes. The full menu is available from opening at 11 AM through midnight, subject to item availability.

What nights have karaoke?

Karaoke is typically offered four nights a week. Days and times may change, so check the live updates or Flespy’s Facebook page before heading over.

Does Flespy’s have an open mic?

Yes. Flespy’s hosts an open mic every Sunday. Check the latest updates for the current start time.

What is Fish Friday?

Fish meals are $1 off every Friday, including fish tacos and fish & chips.

What should I order first?

The fish tacos and chicken wings are two house favorites. Try the battered Alaskan cod tacos with house-made coleslaw, or choose your wing sauce and make a basket disappear.

When are Taco Days?

Taco Days are Tuesday and Thursday from 11 AM to midnight. Crunchy corn tacos are $1.50 each, and soft flour tacos are $2.50 each.

Is the patio open year-round?

Yes. The covered patio is set up for every season, with heating, cooling, televisions, games, and plenty of seating.

Can I order food to go?

Yes. Order through DoorDash for available pickup or delivery options. Current timing, fees, and availability are shown during checkout.

Where can I find special events and schedule updates?

Check the live updates above or visit Flespy’s Facebook page for DJs, live music, parties, and last-minute changes.
