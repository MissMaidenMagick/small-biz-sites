Before you head over

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about food, events, the patio and ordering to go.

What are Flespy’s hours?

Flespy’s is open seven days a week from 11 AM to midnight.

When does the kitchen close?

The complete food menu is served until midnight every day, subject to item availability.

What nights have karaoke?

Karaoke is typically offered four nights a week. Check the events page for the current lineup.

Does Flespy’s have an open mic?

Yes. Open mic is every Sunday. Confirm the current start time in the latest updates.

What are the weekly food specials?

Taco Days are Tuesday and Thursday. Fish meals are $1 off every Friday.

Is the patio open year-round?

Yes. The covered patio stays warm in winter and cool in summer, with an outdoor pool table, giant Connect Four, televisions and seating.

Does Flespy’s have Oregon Lottery?

Yes. Flespy’s has a dedicated Oregon Video Lottery room for guests 21 and over.

Can I order food to go?

Yes. Order through DoorDash for available pickup or delivery options.
