Four nights a week

Karaoke

Grab the mic, pick a song and let the room handle the backup vocals. The exact schedule may change, so confirm the current nights before heading over.

Bring your voice, instrument, comedy, poetry or whatever you are ready to share. Open mic starts at 8 PM every Sunday.

Taco Days

Ground beef or pulled chicken. Crunchy corn tacos are $1.50 and soft flour tacos are $2.50, served from 11 AM until midnight.

Get $1 off fish meals, including battered Alaskan cod tacos and fish and chips. It is a very good way to start the weekend.
